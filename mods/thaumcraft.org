* Entities
** Flux Rift
 - Max size from natural growth is 100
 - But size when spawned from too much flux is \(\sqrt{flux * 3}\)
 - When collapsed via a causaulity collapser, a rift drops
   - \(\frac{\sqrt{size}}{100}\) Primordial Pearls
   - \(\sqrt{size}\) Void Seeds
* Tile Entities
** Essentia Smeltery
 - Alumentum multiplies time between slurry processings by 0.8x
 - The time to smelt an item is 2 ticks per point of essentia in an item being smelted
   - That time is reduced by 0.125x for each bellows affecting the smeltery
     (multiplied by 1 - 0.125n, where n is number of bellows)
   - Bellows only affect the smeltery when attached to the 3 sides in the xz
     plane (horizontally) that aren't the front
 - Efficency is the chance for each point of essentia to be lost as flux when the
   item is smelted
   - Vitium essentia multiplies efficency by 0.66x instead
 - The Smeltery can store up to 256 points of essentia slurry
   - The Smeltery won't smelt an item if it'd cause it to have more than that
     much essentia slurry
   - As a consequence, it can never smelt any item with more than 256 essentia
| Type     | Speed (t) | Efficiency |

| Basic    |        15 |        0.8 |
| Thaumium |        10 |        0.9 |
| Void     |        15 |       0.95 |
