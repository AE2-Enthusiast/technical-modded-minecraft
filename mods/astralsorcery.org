** Rock/Celestial Crystals (EntityCrystal)
 - After 1200 ticks (1:00) of being in liquid starlight
   - has a 1 in 300 chance every tick to
     - increase in size, or
     - spawn a gem cluster
* Liquid Starlight
 - Creates ice when touching fluid colder or equal to water
 - Creates sand when touching fluid otherwise
   - 1 in 900 chance of making Aquamarine Shale instead of sand
* Tile Entities
** Crafting Altar
 - 5% of current starlight is passively lost every tick
 - The altar passively gains starlight every tick as well
   - Above y-level 120:
     - starlight gain is multiplied by \(1 + \frac{y - 120}{272}\)
   - Below or equal to y-level 120:
     - starlight gain is multiplied by \(\frac{y - 20}{100}\) instead
   - Below y-level 40, no starlight is gained
   - Starlight gain is multiplied by \(0.6 + (0.4 * posDistribution)\)
     - where posDistribution is the starfield distribution of the current block pos
   - Starlight gain is multipied by \(0.2 + (0.8 * daytimeDistribution)\)
     - where daytimeDistribution is the current time-based multiplier to
       starlight

* Perks
** Root Perks
*** Armara
 - Gains x xp per hit, where
   - x is damage taken, multiplied by
     - if source is fire damage, then
       - .01 if in lava
       - .2 otherwise
     - .1 if the source is STARVE
     - .05 if source is DROWN
     - .01 if source is CACTUS
     - 1.3 if source is EntityDamageSource
** Key Perks
*** Cull Attack
- After anything is hit
- if it was hit by a player
- and if it had x% health left, where
  - health left if current / max
  - and x is .15 * perk increases*
- the entity's health is set to 0
*** Bastion of Flesh
 - Converts all perk modifiers of armor into modifiers of health instead
 - Sets armor to zero, as well
