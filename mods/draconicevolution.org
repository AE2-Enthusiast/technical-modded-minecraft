* TileEntities
** Crafting Injector
 - Takes at least `chargeSpeedModifier` ticks to fully charge
   - The RF/t input on each injector is limited to \(\mathtext{RF Cost} * \frac{1}{\mathtext{chargeSpeedModifier}}\)
 -
| Name     | Tier | `chargeSpeedModifier` |
|----------+------+-----------------------|
| Basic    |    0 |                   300 |
| Wyvern   |    1 |                   220 |
| Draconic |    2 |                   140 |
| Chaotic  |    3 |                    60 |

 - every tick does something based off of current state
